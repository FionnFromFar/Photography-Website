About Me

Welcome to my portfolio! I am a Physics Student from the University of Bristol on a year abroad exchange at HKUST. Whilst I'm here, I want to learn as much as I possibly can about the unique culture that Hong Kong has: I feel that the most effective way to do so as an individual is through photography. Through photography I gain a unique perspective of not only the city but its people. I intend this site to tell a story of how my trusty, pocketable rx100 and I experience this amazing city ive found myself in for the next year!
