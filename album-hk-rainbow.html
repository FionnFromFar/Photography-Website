HK Rainbow

For this set, I was experimenting with colour and motion blur a lil bit. I took some pretty obvious inspiration from the amazing Wong Kar-wai in the third and fourth shot where the 4th was taken in a road tunnel here in Hong Kong just like the famous shot from his film Fallen Angels. The Third shot was inspired, perhaps less obviously, by Wong Kar-wai again through the motion blur to show the bustle that vibrant Hong Kong so effortlessly portrays. The photos are organised in a rainbow series starting from Red to Pink/Purple because I feel that really emphasised the rich variation of colour in Hong Kong.
